What is BMI?
BMI (Body Mass Index) is a simple health screening number calculated from your weight and height to tell if your weight is in a healthy range.
Underweight Less than 18.5
Normal Weight 18.5 – 24.9
Overweight 25.0 – 29.9
Obesity 30.0 or greater
